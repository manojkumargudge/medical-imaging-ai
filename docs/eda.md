# NIH ChestX-ray14: EDA of the splits

**For education only, not for clinical use.**

Generated by `python -m src.data.eda` from `data/splits/*.csv`; do not edit by hand.

**Labels are NLP-mined, not radiologist-verified.** The 14 findings and No Finding were extracted from radiology reports by text mining. Expect label noise, and treat every metric on these labels as agreement with the mined labels, not with a diagnosis.

Splits: the official `test_list.txt` is the test split; val is about 10% of the `train_val_list.txt` patients (seed 42). No patient is in two splits. All tables count images unless they say patients.
